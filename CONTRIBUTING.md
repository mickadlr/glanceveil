# Contributing to Kalima

Bug reports, compatibility reports, and focused design discussion are welcome
through GitHub Issues. Use synthetic content in reproductions and follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for vulnerabilities.

Kalima is proprietary source-available software. The published source is
provided for inspection and release verification, not as an open-source
collaboration grant. Do not submit code, modified builds, derivative packs, or
pull requests unless the copyright holder has requested that contribution and
agreed to its terms in writing first. Opening an issue does not grant rights
under the source license.
